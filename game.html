The Organized Game

Subbuteo table soccer is played by around 10,000 players in 47 countries, from local clubs to a World Cup every year.

~10,000Players worldwide
47Countries
WeeklyLocal, national and international tournaments
1World Cup every year
How it works

From your club to the world

Players belong to local clubs, like ours. Clubs are overseen by their national association, and national associations come together under the world governing body, FISTF.

FISTF
Federation International Sports Table Football

The world governing body. Its aims:

  • Promote table football worldwide
  • Endorse events around the world
  • Build relationships between national associations
  • Manage the sport's global growth
  • Ensure fair play and settle disputes
fistf.com
WASPA
World Amateur Subbuteo Players Association

An alternative circuit that aims to bring players together around the world and organize tournaments for them.

WASPA on Facebook
ASA
American Subbuteo Association

The national association for the USA, which runs the US national championships.

asa-tablesoccer.com
