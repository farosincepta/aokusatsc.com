Who we are

A Subbuteo club with roots in Koropi

AO Koropiou USA Table Soccer Club plays Subbuteo, the table football game where you flick small figures to pass, shoot and save, in DuPage County, Illinois.

We were founded on October 23, 2026 to do two things: play the game we love, and carry the name of AO Koropiou, Koropi's football club since 1903, to the USA. Beginners are welcome, and we teach the game to anyone who wants to learn.

Our club in Greece

We promote AO Koropiou

AO Koropiou is the football club of Koropi, in East Attica, Greece, playing since 1903. Its name, crest and colors are ours too. Follow the team's results, news and academy on the official website and on Facebook.
